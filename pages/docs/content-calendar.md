## Publishing Workflow

### Content Creation Pipeline

```
Week N: JoC Writer creates 5 articles
  ↓
Week N+1: JoC SEO reviews for keyword optimization, meta tags, schema
  ↓
Week N+1: JoC Visual creates featured images & infographics
  ↓
Week N+2: JoC Publisher schedules & publishes to Odoo CMS
  ↓
Week N+2: JoC SEO verifies live page, submits to Search Console
```

### Quality Checklist Per Article

```
□ Title tag (50-60 chars) with primary keyword
□ Meta description (150-160 chars) with keyword + CTA
□ H1 tag (one per page) with primary keyword
□ H2-H3 structure with secondary keywords
□ Word count: 800-2,000 words (by type)
□ Internal links: 3-5 contextual links to related articles
□ External links: 1-2 authoritative sources (PEROSI, WHO, etc.)
□ Alt text on all images
□ FAQ section with FAQPage schema
□ CTA with WhatsApp deep link (https://wa.me/628811118911)
□ Author/medical reviewer byline
□ "Last updated" timestamp
□ JSON-LD structured data (Article, FAQPage, or MedicalWebPage)
□ Readability check (Flesch-Kincaid appropriate for Indonesian)
□ Mobile responsiveness check
```
