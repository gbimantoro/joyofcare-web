## GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Strategy

AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) favor:

1. **Comprehensive, cited guides** → Create pillar pages (keywords #4, #7, #14, #20)
2. **Pricing transparency** → Publish actual prices in structured format (#5, #11, #12)
3. **FAQ schema markup** → Add to all service and blog pages
4. **Expert authority signals** → Include doctor/therapist credentials in articles
5. **Local business structured data** → Implement LocalBusiness schema on homepage + service pages
6. **Fresh, dated content** → Include "Updated September 2026" on all articles
